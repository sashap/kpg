# 🎮 Kira and Papa Games

A little collection of games we're building together — just for fun! Pick one below and start playing, no installs needed.

## Games

### 🩷 Guardians of the Garden 🦎
`guardians-of-the-garden.html`

**Tower Defense** · 99 Waves · Co-op Friends

Ants, aphids, mosquitoes, caterpillars and snails are invading the garden! Team up Axolotl and Lizard next to each other for a Best Friends bonus, then unlock Spider, Ladybug, Bee and Worm friends as you earn coins. Place them wisely, upgrade your favorites, and see how far through the 99 waves you can get.

### 💗 Splat Battle: Axolotl vs. Lizard 🦎
`axolotl-vs-lizard.html`

**Artillery** · 1 or 2 Players · Silly Fun

A goofy backyard artillery duel! Axolotl and Lizard take turns lobbing mud balls, pine cones, acorns and water balloons across randomly generated hills. Watch the wind, dodge the rain, land hits to unlock sillier ammo, and don't run out of hearts before your rival does.

## Playing

Open `index.html` in a browser to see the game hub, or open either game file directly. Everything is plain HTML/CSS/JS — no build step, no installs.

This is just a fun side project, made with ❤️.
