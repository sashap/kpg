# 🎮 Kira and Papa Games

A little collection of games we're building together — just for fun! Pick one below and start playing, no installs needed.

## Games

### 🩷 Guardians of the Garden 🦎
`guardians-of-the-garden.html`

**Tower Defense** · 999 Waves · Co-op Friends

Ants, aphids, mosquitoes, caterpillars and snails are invading the garden — plus a giant Squirrel boss every 10th wave! Team up Axolotl and Gecko next to each other for a Best Friends bonus, then unlock Spider, Ladybug, Bee and Worm friends as you earn coins. Place them wisely, upgrade your favorites, and see how far through the 999 waves you can get.

### 💗 Splat Battle: Axolotl vs. Lizard 🦎
`axolotl-vs-lizard.html`

**Artillery** · 1 or 2 Players · Silly Fun

A goofy backyard artillery duel! Axolotl and Lizard take turns lobbing mud balls, pine cones, acorns and water balloons across randomly generated hills. Watch the wind, dodge the rain, land hits to unlock sillier ammo, and don't run out of hearts before your rival does.

### 🫐 Blueberry Escape 🥣
`blueberry-escape.html`

**Platformer** · 5 Levels · Pick Your Berry

You're a blueberry, and someone is about to bake with you! Pick purple, dark blue or light blue — each rolls, jumps and floats a little differently — then survive five escapes:

1. **✋ The Counter** — hazards come in alternating waves. A hand grabs from above (its shadow shows where, so dodge sideways), then a hand swipes in from the side aimed at your height (a red band shows where, so jump or drop out of it). Climb the butcher block, toaster, kettle, canisters, spice shelves, tea shelves and window sill to change height fast — and don't fall in the sink, or get flattened by a runaway plate.
2. **🌀 The Blender** — hop up frozen-berry footholds and measuring marks while full-width blades sweep the jar. When a blade is out there's no way past, so wait for it to pull in — and watch for the motor surging. Meanwhile the purée keeps rising, and it accelerates.
3. **🍯 The Pot** — land on a stirring spoon and it flings you higher. Climb out through one of the holes in the pot lid, but one hole hisses and glows before it blasts scalding steam, so take a different one. Then hop the rails to the open window.
4. **🍽️ The Plate** — a top-down dodge arena inspired by Google Doodle Celebrating Popcorn! Run around a giant white porcelain breakfast plate dodging falling and incoming ingredients. Sticky honey and gooey yogurt create puddles that slow you down, crunchy granola clusters fly in all directions like salt bursts, and ripe raspberries pulse and explode into seeds. Hop over gooey puddles and low projectiles, survive the breakfast storm, and hop onto the silver spoon at the rim to catapult to freedom!
5. **🚜 The Farm** — a sprawling, challenging side-scrolling finale! Run from left to right along an expansive sun-drenched country road, hopping across wooden fences, high hay bale pyramids, and fieldstone walls against a backdrop of four multi-layered parallax rolling hills. Dodge larger, jumping squirrels, giant black crows that swoop in from the open sky, angry honking geese that charge on the road, and multiple colossal vintage tractors (Big Red Farmall, Giant Green John Deere, and Colossal Blue Ford) chugging down the lanes. Journey past the big red barn, cows, sheep, and pig pen, and return safely home to the blueberry patch!

Collect the spinning gold coins along the way for bonus points. The dropdown on the start screen lets you jump straight to any level.

### Foil Academy
[Play Foil Academy](foil-academy.html)

**3D Wingfoiling** · 19 Courses · 5 Locations · Keyboard & Touch

Learn to taxi, lift onto the hydrofoil, balance, pump, turn, and land aerial tricks. Practice in Academy Bay or explore recreations of Shoreline Park Lake, 3rd Avenue, Coyote Point, and Crissy Field. Free Foiling lets you choose the wind, waves, weather, and up to 25 other foilers. Swim ashore to explore, visit the castle ruins in Academy Bay, or find Clover and Willow for a bonus horse ride. Push the right joystick farther to progress from walking to galloping.

Play with a laptop keyboard or the two touch controllers on an iPad or iPhone. The single `foil-academy.html` includes its 3D engine, artwork, and sound, so it needs no subfolder files or internet connection after loading. On iPad/iPhone, open it as a webpage in Safari rather than a Files preview. See the [Foil Academy guide](foil-school/README.md) for controls, local hosting, scenery references, and development instructions. The `foil-school/` folder contains source files; the old `foil-school.html` duplicate is no longer needed.
