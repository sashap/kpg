🫐 Blueberry Escape 🥣

You're a blueberry on the kitchen counter, and someone is about to bake with you! Dodge the grabbing hand, climb out of the blender, ride the spoons up through the pot lid and survive the breakfast storm on the plate — four escapes stand between you and freedom.

❓ How to Play

  1. Pick your blueberry, then move with ← → (or A / D) and jump with SPACE, ↑, or W. Hold jump longer to jump higher.
  2. On a touch screen: press a finger anywhere on the left half and slide it left or right to steer — the stick appears wherever you touched. Touch anywhere on the right half to jump, holding for a higher jump. You can steer and jump at the same time.
  3. Level 1 — The Counter: hazards come in waves that alternate. On a grab wave, a shadow shows where the hand will land — it reaches any height, so run out of the shadow sideways.
  4. On a swipe wave, a hand sweeps sideways through one band of height, and a flashing red band shows exactly where a second beforehand. It says GET UP HIGH or DUCK DOWN LOW — do what it says, or jump the hand.
  5. Climb the butcher block, toaster, kettle, canisters and shelves to change height fast. Don't fall in the sink!
  6. Watch for the runaway plate rolling across the counter — hop over it. It ends up in the sink.
  7. Level 2 — The Blender: the blades spin flat, so each looks like a bar that stretches out and pulls back in. When one is out it blocks the whole jar and there is no way past — wait on your mark.
  8. When a blade pulls in you get a green ring: that is your window, jump then. Red wash means stay put.
  9. Climb your own side of the glass, and cross to the other side where there is no blade in the way. Miss a jump and you'll drop back onto the blade below.
  10. The purée keeps rising, so don't linger — climb over the rim to escape.
  11. Level 3 — The Pot: land on a stirring spoon and it will fling you upward. Chain the launches to reach the lid.
  12. The pot is lidded, so climb out through one of the steam holes — but one hole hisses and glows before it blasts scalding steam, so take a different one. Then hop to the open window to win!
  13. Level 4 — The Plate: a top-down dodge arena inspired by Google Doodle Celebrating Popcorn! Run around the giant white circular plate dodging a breakfast storm.
  14. Sticky honey and gooey yogurt leave puddles that slow you down, crunchy granola flies in all directions like salt, and plump raspberries swell and explode into juice and seed shrapnel!
  15. Controls: Move in all directions with ← → ↑ ↓ or W A S D (or slide the left stick on touch). Press SPACE (or tap the right side on touch) to hop into the air!
  16. Pro-tip: Hop over gooey puddles and jump right over flying granola to avoid damage!
  17. Survive the breakfast rush, then hop onto the silver spoon at the rim to catapult to sweet freedom!
  18. You lose a heart each time you're caught. Lose them all and it's back to the mixing bowl.
  19. Grab the spinning gold coins along the way — each one is worth points.
