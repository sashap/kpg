🫐 Blueberry Escape 🥣

You're a blueberry on the kitchen counter, and someone is about to bake with you! Dodge the grabbing hand, climb out of the blender, and ride the spoons past the stove — three escapes stand between you and the open window.

❓ How to Play

  1. Pick your blueberry, then move with ← → (or A / D) and jump with SPACE, ↑, or W. Hold jump longer to jump higher.
  2. Level 1 — The Counter: hazards come in waves that alternate. On a grab wave, a shadow shows where the hand will land — it reaches any height, so run out of the shadow sideways.
  3. On a swipe wave, a hand sweeps sideways through one band of height, and a flashing red band shows exactly where a second beforehand. It says GET UP HIGH or DUCK DOWN LOW — do what it says, or jump the hand.
  4. Climb the butcher block, toaster, kettle, canisters and shelves to change height fast. Don't fall in the sink!
  5. Watch for the runaway plate rolling across the counter — hop over it. It ends up in the sink.
  6. Level 2 — The Blender: the blades spin flat, so each looks like a bar that stretches out and pulls back in. When one is out it blocks the whole jar and there is no way past — wait on your mark.
  7. When a blade pulls in you get a green ring: that is your window, jump then. Red wash means stay put.
  8. Climb your own side of the glass, and cross to the other side where there is no blade in the way. Miss a jump and you'll drop back onto the blade below.
  9. The purée keeps rising, so don't linger — climb over the rim to escape.
  10. Level 3 — The Pot: land on a stirring spoon and it will fling you upward. Chain the launches to reach the lid.
  11. The pot is lidded, so climb out through one of the steam holes — but one hole hisses and glows before it blasts scalding steam, so take a different one. Then hop to the open window to win!
  12. You lose a heart each time you're caught. Lose them all and it's back to the mixing bowl.
  13. Collect ✨ sugar sparkles along the way for bonus points.
